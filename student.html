01 / WHAT IS FRC?

Sport-level energy.
Engineering-level work.

FIRST Robotics Competition is a high-school program for students ages 14–18. Teams receive a Kit of Parts, then design, build, and program an industrial-sized robot for a new game released each January. At events, three-team alliances compete on a themed field while students also run strategy, safety, fundraising, outreach, and team operations.

04 / THE COMMITMENT

Show up.
Make progress.

There is no single national schedule for every team. ECS will publish its own meeting calendar with mentors and families. As a realistic starting point, plan for 2–3 hours per meeting during build season, plus occasional weekend workdays and competitions. Schoolwork, safety, sleep, and family commitments come first.

  1. 01Learn

    Safety, tools, CAD, controls, or team operations.

  2. 02Build

    Turn a prototype into a tested subsystem.

  3. 03Reflect

    Document decisions, failures, and the next test.

06 / AFTER HIGH SCHOOL

Useful for college.
Useful everywhere.

FRC can give you concrete evidence of how you learn: a CAD model, software contribution, tested mechanism, outreach project, or leadership decision. Those artifacts can strengthen a portfolio and give you specific material for applications and interviews. FIRST also maintains scholarship opportunities, but eligibility and awards change—check the current official listings instead of assuming participation guarantees admission or money.
